Our Story

Built on the spice route, headed toward the pantry.

Hotspy started with a simple frustration: most spice on the shelf has been sitting there for years, cut with filler, and stripped of the oils that make it worth using. We began sourcing single-lot spice directly from growers, testing every batch for purity, and packing it close to harvest.

What started as a spice house is the first chapter of something larger — we're building toward a full pantry of everyday FMCG staples, sourced with the same discipline.
