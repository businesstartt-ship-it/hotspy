Our Story

From the Earth
to Your Kitchen

Hotspy was born from a simple belief — that food tastes better when it is made with love and pure ingredients. What started as sharing homemade masala with neighbors in Dhaka has grown into a mission to bring authentic, organic spices to every kitchen in Bangladesh.

Every batch is hand-ground in small quantities to ensure freshness and flavor. We source our ingredients directly from organic farms in Rajshahi, Bogra, and Sylhet, supporting local farmers while delivering unmatched quality.
